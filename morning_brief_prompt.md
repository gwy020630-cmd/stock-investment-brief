# Morning Brief Prompt

Read:
- `portfolio_watchlist.json`
- `daily_brief_spec.md`
- `source_map.md`
- `watchlist_candidates.json`

Then produce the daily brief.

## Rules

- Use current sources only.
- Focus on developments since the previous brief.
- Prioritize official company and primary sources where available.
- Distinguish facts from inference.
- Keep the brief concise, decision-useful, and timed for the UK morning.

## Additional Instructions

- Do not assume the watchlist is fixed.
- Evaluate both current holdings and newly relevant companies.
- Surface positive AI-sector catalysts even if they are not formal partnerships.
- If a candidate appears compelling, explain whether it deserves to be added to the watchlist before the U.S. market opens.
- Connect macro changes to likely moves in AI-related equities.

## Candidate Queue Update

At the end of the brief, if any new candidate companies stand out, summarize them in a format that can be copied into `watchlist_candidates.json` with:
- company name
- ticker if known
- catalyst
- why it matters
- urgency
- date added
