# Source Map

Use primary and official sources first when possible. Use secondary reporting to widen coverage, then confirm material claims with official releases, filings, or transcripts.

## Macro

Priority areas:
- Federal Reserve commentary and meeting materials
- U.S. Treasury yields and broad rate moves
- Inflation, labor, and growth releases
- Dollar, oil, tariffs, export controls, and geopolitical risk

Useful source types:
- central bank releases
- government statistical releases
- exchange and market data pages
- high-quality financial reporting

## Power And Infrastructure

Priority areas:
- nuclear and advanced reactors
- grid equipment
- power management
- cooling and thermal infrastructure
- data center landlords and builders

Watch for:
- data center power agreements
- reactor or energy infrastructure announcements
- utility and grid bottlenecks
- direct links between AI buildout and electricity demand

## Company-Specific Priority Order

When evaluating a holding or candidate, prioritize:
1. investor relations releases
2. earnings materials and transcripts
3. SEC filings or equivalent official filings
4. company newsroom or official blog
5. major financial reporting
6. sector press or industry analysis

## Candidate Discovery Triggers

Potential add-to-watchlist names should be flagged when they have:
- a clear positive AI-sector catalyst,
- direct exposure to AI demand or infrastructure,
- evidence of a meaningful customer, supply, or capacity inflection,
- or a new role in an important AI value chain.
